Title: The Beauty of Life 🌸
1. Life is a journey, a story untold,
2. Filled with adventures, both new and old.
3. The sun will rise after every night,
4. Bringing new hope and shining light.
5. Through every storm, learn to stand tall,
6. For strength is born when we face it all.
7. Dreams are stars that light the way,
8. Guiding our hearts to a brighter day.
9. Never let fear steal your dreams,
10. For life is greater than it sometimes seems.
11. Every failure teaches us to grow,
12. Every hardship helps our courage show.
13. Like flowers blooming after rain,
14. We find our beauty through the pain.
15. Keep your heart gentle, your spirit free,
16. Become the person you're meant to be.
17. Let kindness shine in all you do,
18. And let your actions speak what's true.
19. For life is precious, a gift to embrace,
20. So walk with courage, love, and grace. 🌹
